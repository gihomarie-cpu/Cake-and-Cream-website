More information about Rwanda cakes and creams

Our founder

Marie,the visionary behind Cakes and Cream, discovered her passion for baking during her time at the Catholic university of Rwanda. Starting with simple recipe books and baking cakes as a hobby, she soon realized the potential to turn her love for baking into a thriving business.

Driven by her dedication to perfection and a desire to spread joy through delicious treats, Marie honed her skills and transformed her hobby into a full-succes enterprise. With a focus on quality ingredients, innovative flavors, she established Cakes and Cream as a beloved brand.

Her entrepreneurial spirit and commitment to excellence continue to shape the Cakes and Cream experience, as she leads a team of talented bakers and confectioners in creating unforgettable moments of sweetness.
